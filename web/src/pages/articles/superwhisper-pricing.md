Superwhisper's pricing is unusual among dictation apps in two ways: the free tier is unlimited in volume, and you can buy the paid tier once instead of renting it. This page sets out what each tier includes as of October 2026, from Superwhisper's documentation, and when each makes sense. Prices are in US dollars.

## The tiers

| | Price | What you get |
|---|---|---|
| Free | $0 | Unlimited dictation with the local Whisper models; two modes; custom vocabulary; text replacements |
| Pro, monthly | $8.49 a month | Everything below |
| Pro, yearly | $84.99 a year (about $7.08 a month) | Everything below |
| Pro, lifetime | $249.99 once | Everything below, for good |

New users get 3,000 words of access to the Pro features before choosing.

## What the free tier is really like

More than most free tiers. Every Whisper model is included, from Tiny to Large v3 and Large v3 Turbo, running on your Mac, with no word limit and no account needed. Two modes is enough for plain transcription plus one formatted style. The vocabulary and text replacements cover names and jargon. For someone who wants accurate, private, offline dictation and is happy to tidy the text themselves, the free tier is the product, and it stays free.

What it lacks is the second half of Superwhisper: anything that rewrites your words with a language model, and anything beyond dictation.

## What Pro unlocks

- **AI processing.** Modes that reformat, clean up or transform what you said, through Superwhisper's own language models, cloud models from OpenAI, Anthropic, Google and Groq, your own API keys, or the small local S1-mini model.
- **Unlimited modes**, so each app or task can have its own instructions.
- **More voice models:** the local Parakeet and Parakeet Multilanguage, Cohere Transcribe, and the cloud models (S1-Voice, Nova, Scribe).
- **Context awareness**, which reads what is on screen to inform the clean-up.
- **File transcription** of audio and video.
- **System audio recording**, with speaker separation, for meetings and calls.
- **Realtime transcription** and translation.

If you want your dictation cleaned up by an AI model, you need Pro. That is the dividing line for most buyers.

## Monthly, yearly or lifetime

| Horizon | Monthly | Yearly | Lifetime |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 year | $101.88 | $84.99 | $249.99 |
| 2 years | $203.76 | $169.98 | $249.99 |
| 3 years | $305.64 | $254.97 | $249.99 |
| 5 years | $509.40 | $424.95 | $249.99 |

Lifetime overtakes monthly after about 29 months and yearly after about 35. The usual reasoning applies: lifetime is a bet that the app, and your use of it, will still be around in three years. Superwhisper has shipped since 2023 and is actively developed, which makes the bet more reasonable than for a new app, but it is still a bet. Check, before buying, how many devices and platforms one licence covers; Superwhisper runs on Mac, Windows, iPhone and Android, and the terms for a licence across them are set on its site, not here.

## Against the competition

Wispr Flow's Pro is $15 a month or $144 a year, with no lifetime option and a free plan capped at 2,000 words a week; its free tier is the full product with a meter, Superwhisper's is unlimited volume with fewer features. Apple's Dictation is free with the Mac and does no clean-up. Our own [JustScribe](/superwhisper-alternative) is free with everything included, on-device only, and smaller in scope than Superwhisper Pro. [Wispr Flow vs Superwhisper](/articles/wispr-flow-vs-superwhisper) goes through the first comparison in detail.

## Who should pay

- **Pay nothing** if you want private, offline dictation and will edit the text yourself. The free tier does that without limits.
- **Pay yearly** if you want AI clean-up and are not yet sure the habit will stick.
- **Pay lifetime** if you already dictate every day, will keep doing so, and would rather not have another subscription.
