[MacWhisper](https://www.macwhisper.com) is best known for turning recordings into text: drop in an audio or video file, or record a meeting, and get a transcript. It also has system-wide dictation. JustScribe starts from the other end: dictation first, with a simpler file transcription built in, and all of it free.

## How they compare

| | JustScribe | MacWhisper |
|---|---|---|
| Main job | Dictation into any app | Transcribing files and meetings, plus dictation |
| Price | Free, everything included | Free version, and a one-time Pro licence |
| Source code | Open source (GPL-3.0) | Closed |
| Where transcription runs | On your Mac only | On your Mac, with optional cloud models |
| File transcription | Yes: audio and video, timestamps, speaker labels, plain text | Yes, with more formats |
| Meeting recording | No | Yes |
| Speaker labels | Yes | Yes |
| Subtitles and export formats | No, plain text only | Yes |
| Live text while you speak | Yes | Check its feature list |
| Grammar correction | Yes, on-device | Yes |

MacWhisper details are from [macwhisper.com](https://www.macwhisper.com) as of October 2026; its comparison table shows which features need the Pro licence.

## When JustScribe is the better fit

- **You want to talk instead of type, and nothing else.** JustScribe lives in the menu bar, has one shortcut, and stays out of the way.
- **You want dictation without buying a licence.** All of JustScribe is free.
- **You want an app you can inspect.** The [source](https://github.com/bring-shrubbery/justscribe) is public.

## When MacWhisper is the better fit

- You transcribe many files at once, or need them in a particular format.
- You want meetings recorded and transcribed, with speakers told apart.
- You need subtitles or transcript exports.

Many people will still want both: MacWhisper for meetings and exports, JustScribe for writing by voice and the occasional recording.

## How JustScribe transcribes a file

Choose **Transcribe File…** from the menu bar, drop in a recording or a video, and the transcript appears paragraph by paragraph with a timestamp on each. Turn on *Identify speakers* to label who said what. Copy the result or save it as text. Everything runs on your Mac; see [transcribing audio files on a Mac](/guides/transcribe-audio-files-mac).

## How JustScribe's dictation works

Hold the shortcut and speak. Text appears in the focused app every couple of seconds; when you let go, JustScribe transcribes the whole recording once more and corrects what it typed. With [grammar correction](/guides/grammar-correction) on, it then fixes grammar, spelling and punctuation, still on your Mac. See [offline speech to text on Mac](/guides/offline-speech-to-text-mac) for the models you can choose from.
